Privacy Policy – WevaNeo: AI Prompt Inspiration

Last updated: 14 October 2026

WevaNeo: AI Prompt Inspiration ("WevaNeo", "the app") is a gallery of AI image and video prompts. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: WevaNeo does not require an account and does not collect, sell or share personal information.

1. Information we do not collect

The app has no sign-up or login. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any other personal data, and it contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

2. Information stored only on your device

To work, the app keeps the following on your device. It never leaves your device and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it:

  • your favourites, collections and viewing history;
  • recent searches;
  • settings (theme, card size, notification time, content filter);
  • cached copies of posts and images, so the app loads faster.

3. Network requests

The app downloads content (prompts and images/videos) from the public WevaNeo blog (Blogger/Google) and from the image hosts referenced by the posts. Like any internet request, these servers can see your IP address and device type as part of the normal connection. WevaNeo does not add any identifier to these requests.

4. On-device translation

The optional "Translate" feature uses Google ML Kit on-device language identification and translation. Translation runs on your device; the language model is downloaded once from Google. The prompt text is not sent to our servers.

5. Notifications and widget

If you enable the daily "Prompt of the day" notification, the app asks for notification permission and schedules a local reminder on your device. The home-screen widget only shows content already available to the app. Both can be turned off at any time.

6. Saving and sharing

When you use "Save", an image or video is written to your gallery (Pictures/WevaNeo or Movies/WevaNeo). When you use "Share" or "Copy", the prompt text is passed to the app you choose. These actions happen only when you request them.

7. Content

Prompts and media in the app are AI-related inspiration collected from public sources, with the creator credited where available. The app includes an optional filter that hides content that may be sensitive; it is a best-effort filter and not a guarantee. The app is intended for users aged 18 and over.

8. Children

WevaNeo is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
