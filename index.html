Privacy Policy

Anime Live Wallpaper HD 4K

Last updated: August 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Anime Live Wallpaper HD 4K. Please read it before using the app.

Information We Collect

The app itself does not collect personal information. You are not asked to provide a name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials in order to use it. There is no registration, no sign-in, and no user profile.

The app does display advertising, and our advertising partner collects certain device information. This is described in the Advertising section below.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your favourites list is saved only on your own device. It is never uploaded to any server, and it is removed completely when you uninstall the app.

Wallpapers you open are held temporarily in a local cache so that browsing feels faster. Live wallpaper videos you choose to apply are saved in the app's private storage. Both can be cleared at any time from your device settings, and both are removed when the app is uninstalled.

Advertising

This app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Google and its partners may collect and use device information, including the Advertising ID, IP address, and general information about how you interact with ads, in order to serve, measure, and improve advertising. Depending on your choices and your region, advertising may be personalised based on inferred interests.

This information is shared with Google as a third party. The app does not send Google any of the content you view or any information you have typed.

You can read how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your Advertising Choices

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, a consent dialog is shown the first time you open the app. You may change your answer at any time using the privacy settings button that appears at the top of the app's main screen.

Regardless of where you are located, you can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised advertising from your device settings, usually under Settings, then Google, then Ads.

Permissions

Internet access is used to load wallpaper images and videos, and to load advertisements.

Set wallpaper is used only at the moment you choose to apply a wallpaper.

Storage is used, on older Android versions, only when you choose to download a file to your gallery. On Android 10 and above, no storage permission is requested.

The advertising identifier permission is added automatically by the Google Mobile Ads library and is used only for advertising purposes as described above.

Third-Party Services

Wallpaper content is delivered from third-party hosting and content delivery services. As with any website you visit, those providers may record technical information such as your IP address as part of ordinary server operation. The app itself does not send them any information about you.

Advertising is provided by Google AdMob, as described in the Advertising section. The app contains no analytics software.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under the age of 16, and no personal information is knowingly collected from anyone. If you believe a child has provided information through this app, please contact us so it can be removed.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any revision will be published on this page and the date above will be changed accordingly.
