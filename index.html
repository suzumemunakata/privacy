Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Anime Live Wallpaper HD 4K (the "Application"). Please read it before using the Application.

Information We Collect

The Application does not collect personal information. You are not asked to provide a name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials in order to use it. There is no registration, no sign-in, and no user profile.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your favourites list is saved only on your own device. It is never uploaded to any server, and it is removed completely when you uninstall the Application.

Wallpapers you open are held temporarily in a local cache so that browsing feels faster. Live wallpaper videos you choose to apply are saved in the Application's private storage. Both can be cleared at any time from your device settings, and both are removed when the Application is uninstalled.

Permissions

  • Internet access — used to load wallpaper images and videos.
  • Set wallpaper — used only at the moment you choose to apply a wallpaper.
  • Storage — on older Android versions, used only when you choose to download a file to your gallery. On Android 10 and above, no storage permission is requested.

Third-Party Services

Wallpaper content is delivered from third-party hosting and content delivery services. As with any website you visit, those providers may record technical information such as your IP address as part of ordinary server operation. The Application itself does not send them any information about you.

The Application contains no advertising and no analytics software.

Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed at children under the age of 16, and no personal information is knowingly collected from anyone.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any revision will be published on this page and the date above will be changed accordingly.
